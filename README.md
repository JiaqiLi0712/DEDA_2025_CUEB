# 全球电动汽车市场发展趋势分析（2010-2024）

## 项目简介

本项目基于国际能源署（International Energy Agency, IEA）数据集以及《Global EV Outlook 2023》报告，对2010年至2024年全球电动汽车（Electric Vehicle, EV）市场发展趋势进行数据分析。

项目通过数据可视化、聚类分析、主题建模和回归分析等方法，研究全球电动汽车市场增长趋势、不同国家和地区的市场类型、政策影响以及技术因素。

分析结果显示，全球电动汽车市场在过去十余年快速增长，其中中国在电动汽车销量和市场规模方面处于领先地位。

通过 K-means 聚类和层次聚类分析，全球电动车市场被划分为四种主要类型：

- **领先市场（Leaders）**：中国
- **快速追随市场（Fast Followers）**：欧盟、美国
- **新兴市场（Emerging Markets）**：以色列、土耳其等
- **滞后市场（Laggards）**：电动车普及速度较慢的地区

此外，LDA主题模型识别出影响电动汽车发展的关键主题，包括电池技术、电动车政策和市场支持工具。回归分析进一步验证了政策支持对电动车渗透率提升的重要作用，同时电池技术的发展也促进了电动车销量增长。

研究结果表明，新兴市场仍存在充电基础设施不足的问题，而有效的政策措施能够显著推动电动汽车市场的发展。


## 分析方法

# Global Electric Vehicle Market Trend Analysis (2010-2024)

## Description

This project analyzes global electric vehicle (EV) market trends from 2010 to 2024 based on International Energy Agency (IEA) datasets and the *Global EV Outlook 2023* report.

The project applies data visualization, clustering analysis, topic modeling, and regression analysis to investigate global EV adoption trends, market segmentation, policy impacts, and technological factors across different countries and regions.

The analysis demonstrates rapid global EV adoption growth, with China leading electric vehicle sales and market expansion.

Using K-means clustering and hierarchical clustering methods, global EV markets are categorized into four major groups:

- **Leaders**: China
- **Fast Followers**: European Union and the United States
- **Emerging Markets**: Israel, Türkiye, and other developing markets
- **Laggards**: Regions with slower EV adoption

Furthermore, LDA topic modeling identifies major themes influencing EV development, including battery technology, EV policies, and market support tools. Regression analysis confirms that policy support significantly improves EV penetration rates, while advances in battery technology contribute to increasing EV sales.

The findings highlight infrastructure gaps in emerging markets and demonstrate the effectiveness of policy interventions in accelerating global EV adoption.

## Methodology

The following analytical methods are applied in this project:

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data Visualization

- Hierarchical Clustering
